01 / PROFILE

About me

Hello, I’m Pumudu Rajakaruna, a Computer Engineering graduate from the University of Peradeniya with two years of industry experience in software engineering, complemented by research experience in machine learning and applied data science.

My work spans software engineering, research, and applied data science, with a focus on machine learning and clinical decision support. I have contributed to real-world systems in fintech and mobile banking, while also engaging in interdisciplinary healthcare research and academic collaboration.

I am strongly interested in teaching, research, and academic activities, with experience in laboratory instruction, published work in health analytics, and participation in collaborative research projects that bridge engineering and applied problem solving.
